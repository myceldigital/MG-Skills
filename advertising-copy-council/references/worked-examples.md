# Worked examples

These are invented training briefs and original sample copy, not historic ads or evidence of campaign effectiveness.
Do not transfer their names, facts, terms, or results into unrelated assignments.

## Same product, different readiness and medium

**Fictional brief:** LedgerFollow serves freelancers who manually chase invoices.
It sends email reminders on a user-selected schedule and pauses reminders when the user marks an invoice paid.
There is a free trial with no card required; no trial duration is supplied.
There is no evidence of faster customer payment, measured time savings, automatic payment detection, or superior performance.

### Problem-focused opening

**Copy:**

> Still writing "Just following up on my invoice"?
>
> LedgerFollow sends the reminders you schedule, so you can stop writing the same follow-up email each time.
> Mark an invoice paid, and its reminders pause.
>
> Try LedgerFollow free. No card required.

**Decision:** A recognisable situation introduces a supported capability.
The copy preserves the user's manual payment-marking step and makes no payment-speed promise.

### Product-aware headline and CTA

**Copy:**

> Schedule your invoice reminders with LedgerFollow.
>
> Start your free trial. No card required.

**Decision:** A familiar-product audience can receive a more direct invitation.
No invented trial duration is needed.

### Short search assets

**Headline:** Schedule Invoice Reminders

**Description:** Set reminder dates. Mark invoices paid to pause reminders. Try LedgerFollow free.

**Decision:** The assets explain the supported action without depending on a story.
Check actual limits before deployment; these examples do not assert platform compliance.

### Proposed video treatment

Show an invoice list, a schedule being selected, and reminders pausing after a user marks an invoice paid.
The narration explains those actions.
This is a proposed product demonstration to verify against the real interface, not an assertion that footage exists.
Do not show the customer's bank balance rising or a paid invoice appearing automatically.

### Tempting but unsupported draft

> Get paid twice as fast with the world's smartest AI invoice system.
> Start your 30-day trial before midnight.

**Problems:** Unsupported result magnitude, superiority, AI capability, duration, and deadline.
Replace with verified functionality, not weaker-looking versions of the same unsupported claims.

## Technical buyers need the feature

**Fictional brief:** GaugeOne records temperature once per second, exports CSV, and operates from 0 to 60 degrees Celsius.
Audience: lab technicians evaluating data logging.
No measurement-accuracy or regulatory-compliance evidence is supplied.

**Copy:**

> A temperature reading every second. A CSV when you need the data.
>
> GaugeOne records temperature once per second and exports readings in CSV format for analysis in your chosen tools.
> Operating range: 0 to 60 degrees Celsius.

**Decision:** The interval, export format, and range help the buyer assess fit.
Do not call this "laboratory-grade accuracy" or promise compatibility with named software without evidence.

## A quiet brand brief does not require an offer

**Fictional brief:** Stillroom makes a reading chair with a solid oak frame and a linen cover.
Audience: design-conscious homeowners.
Request: a restrained print headline and one supporting line, with no CTA or discount.

**Copy:**

> Make room for another chapter.
>
> The Stillroom reading chair, with a solid oak frame and linen cover.

**Decision:** A use moment provides emotional relevance while the supporting line identifies the product and materials.
Do not add handmade provenance, sustainability claims, comfort guarantees, limited editions, or a promotion.

## A narrow edit remains narrow

**Request:** Rewrite only "Our solution leverages automation to optimise invoice management" in plain English.
The supplied capability is scheduled invoice reminders.

**Copy:**

> Schedule your invoice reminders.

**Decision:** A single accurate sentence completes the request.
No council transcript, compulsory questionnaire, or expanded sales page is needed.
